# Privacy Policy — Khabo Ki?

_Last updated: 3 November 2026_

Khabo Ki? ("the app") helps you check food freshness, nutrition and safety from a photo. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Food photos you choose to scan** | To analyze the food | Sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini AI. **We do not store your photos.** |
| **A random device ID** | Only to limit how many scans one device can make per minute (to prevent abuse) | Created on your phone; it is not linked to your name, phone number or account |
| **Your IP address** | Rate limiting, handled by Cloudflare | Not stored by us |

We do **not** collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or any account information. There are no ads and no analytics trackers.

## Scan history

Your scan history stays **on your phone only**. It is cleared when you close the app or tap "Clear history".

## Third-party services

- **Google Gemini API** analyzes the photo. We use Google's free tier, under which **Google may use submitted content to improve its products**. Please do not scan photos that show people or private information. See [Google's terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms).
- **Cloudflare Workers** runs our server. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Important health notice

Results are based on a photo only. The app **cannot detect bacteria, chemicals or formalin** and is not a substitute for professional food testing or medical advice. When in doubt, throw it out.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes and contact

We will update this page if anything changes. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nuhanhabib/khabo-ki/issues.
